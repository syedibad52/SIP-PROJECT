# ♻️ PlastoForge 3D – Plastic to Filament

Transforming waste PET bottles into affordable, high-quality 3D printing filament through sustainable manufacturing.

## 📌 Overview

PlastoForge 3D is an innovative sustainability-focused project that converts discarded PET plastic bottles into reusable 3D printer filament. The project aims to reduce plastic waste, promote circular manufacturing, and make 3D printing more affordable for students, makerspaces, educational institutions, and small businesses.

By recycling PET waste into usable filament, PlastoForge 3D contributes to environmental conservation while creating a low-cost alternative to commercially available filament.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Reduce PET plastic waste entering landfills and waterways.
* Produce affordable 3D printing filament from recycled materials.
* Promote sustainable manufacturing practices.
* Support educational institutions and makerspaces with low-cost filament solutions.
* Encourage a circular economy approach to plastic usage.

---

## 🔄 Process Workflow

### 1. Collection & Stripping

* Used PET bottles are collected.
* Bottles are cleaned thoroughly.
* Plastic is cut into thin strips for processing.

### 2. Heating & Extrusion

* PET strips are heated to approximately **210°C**.
* Molten plastic is pushed through a precision extrusion nozzle.
* Continuous filament is formed.

### 3. Spooling

* The extruded filament is cooled.
* Filament is wound uniformly onto standard reels.

### 4. Testing & Packaging

* Filament diameter and quality are tested.
* Finished filament is packaged for end users.
